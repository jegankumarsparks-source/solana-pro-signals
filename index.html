How signals work

  1. A call is posted. It names the token, contract and the levels being watched.
  2. You do your own checks. Liquidity, contract authorities and holder concentration.
  3. You decide. Trade it, skip it or just watch. It is entirely your call.
  4. Milestones are posted when a call moves up. Calls that fail don't get a milestone, so don't judge the group by milestones alone.
